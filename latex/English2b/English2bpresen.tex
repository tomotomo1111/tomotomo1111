\section{About The Previous Study Manuscript}
\subsection{Page 3}
%
I will talk about the means and methods of previous research. 
In that study, the subjects were classified in descending order of 
the degree of contact with foreigners as "no contact," "contact 
opportunities only," "greeting contact only," "passive contact only," 
and "active contact," 
\subsection{Page 4}
and asked whether they agreed or disagreed with 
the increase in foreigners in society.
\subsection{Page 5}
The result is shown in the pie chart on the left.
\subsection{Page 6}
It was found that the majority of respondents were more opposed 
than in favor; those with "active contact" were more likely to be in favor; 
those with "greeting contact only" and "passive contact only" were slightly 
more likely to be in favor; while those with "contact opportunities only" 
and "no contact" were very likely to be opposed.
\subsection{Page 7}
At the time the data for this previous study was obtained in 2003, 
opposition to the idea greatly outweighed approval, 
probably due to the fact that society as a whole did not 
have sufficient opportunities to come into contact with foreigners.
